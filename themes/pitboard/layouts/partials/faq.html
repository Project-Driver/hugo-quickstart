Where does the data come from?

Your own GoHighLevel sub-account: the phone system, inbox, calendars, pipelines and invoices Project Driver installs with Never Miss a Job and AI Front Desk. Pit Board reads it with a private integration token you can revoke at any time. Nothing is copied out; the board is built each morning and sent from your own number and email.

I do not have GoHighLevel. Can I still get it?

Yes, on Crew or Pro. The setup (missed-call text-back, one inbox, booking calendar) is included, and it is what puts the numbers on the board in the first place. Board alone needs an existing account.

What is "money on the table"?

Four things, ranked: missed calls that never got a text back, customers whose last message nobody answered, estimates sent more than three days ago that have not booked, and invoices that are unpaid or overdue. Each one is a link that opens the conversation on your phone.

What is the response score?

A single number for the day. Sixty percent is the share of missed calls that got a text back within ten minutes. Forty percent is how fast the first reply went out to customers who texted. Under 50 means people are calling someone else.

What time does it arrive?

7:00 AM in your time zone by text and email. The Monday edition adds the last seven days. On Crew and Pro, the monthly pit-stop call walks the whole month by source.

Can I stop it?

Reply STOP to the text or cancel from the email footer. Month to month, no term on Board or Crew. Pro includes the AI receptionist and runs on a three-month minimum, the same as the receptionist on its own.

Does my office manager get it too?

Yes. Any number of recipients on the same account. Most owners send it to themselves and whoever runs the front desk.
